Design language

Quiet studio with warm paper. Five rules that decide every later argument.

1 · Text is the only loud thing

Chrome sits at --dimmed or below. Nothing competes with prose for attention. Accent teal marks state and action, never decoration.

2 · Containers are earned

Whitespace and hairlines before boxes; boxes before shadows. A card exists only where things must be told apart at a glance. Today three card styles wrap things that needed no box at all.

3 · One measure

Prose is 34em ≈ 68 characters wherever it appears — editor, note, reader, preview. The reader already does this at 760px. The editor does not, and that is the single biggest defect in the app.

4 · Chrome retreats on typing

Begin typing and furniture fades. Move the pointer, press Esc, or stop for 3s and it returns. Never removes information you cannot get back instantly.

5 · Display type belongs to the book

Macondo and Playwrite set book titles, chapter headings and prose — the things that are yours. Application chrome gets a quiet face that survives 11px.

Recommendation

Source Sans 3. Humanist rather than geometric, so it sits beside a handwriting face without arguing with it; warmer than Inter, which reads slightly clinical against this palette; and it holds up at 11px uppercase where Fira gets wide and Plex gets technical. Inter is the safe second choice if you'd rather have the more neutral, more familiar option.

Colour moves from a 3px left bar to a 7px dot at the root and a 2px accent on branches — quieter, and it stops reading as a status indicator. Connectors become real orthogonal hairlines instead of implied space. Depth is shown by shrinking type and fading opacity, so a deep tree stays scannable. Costs: horizontal scrolling is unavoidable past ~4 levels, and it does not survive a phone — you keep the current vertical reflow as a second layout. Pays: nothing else looks like this, and the spatial memory is real.
