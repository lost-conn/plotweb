What changed and why
  • The jacket fills all 252px instead of leaving ~190px blank. Title, rule, author — generated, so it looks intentional on day one with zero uploaded art.
  • Spine moved from a flat ::before bar to a gradient with an inner shadow, and the radius is asymmetric (2px at the spine, 8px at the fore-edge) so it reads as a physical book.
  • Meta moved below the jacket rather than floating over its bottom edge, and now carries word count, chapter count, and last-edited. Currently the card shows word count above the title and nothing else.
  • Shared books keep the violet spine but get a "3 chapters unread" line, which is the only thing you actually want to know about a book someone shared with you. Needs read-progress on the shared side — the one model addition kept.
  • Delete moved out of a hover-only naked trash icon into a ⋯ menu — the current one is a single misclick from destroying a manuscript.

Book workspace — content up, tools down

The sidebar becomes what it should always have been: the manuscript. Typography, Beta readers and History drop to an icon strip along the foot, because you open them roughly once a week and they currently carry the same visual weight as the chapter you are writing.

What changed and why
  • Chapters became rows, not cards. Six Paper cards with 16px padding and gaps show six chapters in the space a list shows fifteen. Hairline-separated rows scale to a 40-chapter novel; cards do not.
  • One primary action. Import and Export move behind ⋯ (and open as side sheets). Today Import / Export / Add Chapter are three near-equal buttons above a list you mostly came to read.
  • Tools became a footer strip with tooltips and a count badge on Beta readers. Five flat text labels become four icons, and the sidebar's vertical space goes to the manuscript.
  • Word counts in the sidebar — currently only the main list has them, so the nav you actually use is the one with less information.
  • Drag handle replaces the up/down chevron pair. Reordering a 40-chapter book by clicking ▲ thirty times is not reordering.

What changed and why
  • A measure. Prose is capped at 34em ≈ 68 characters and centred. Today it runs the full ~890px of the pane — roughly 110 characters, well past the point where your eye loses the line return. This is the single biggest defect in the app, and the reader already gets it right at 760px.
  • The formatting toolbar is gone until you select text. Sixteen buttons sit on screen permanently right now, for operations that only mean anything with a selection.
  • The feedback rail is only mounted when it has content. Today it holds 300px open to say "No feedback for this chapter." With it gone, the writing pane gets that width back.
  • Word count and save state moved to a footer that fades with everything else — visible when you look, invisible when you write. The "Saved / Saved on this device" distinction keeps its dot, since that one genuinely matters.
  • Collapse on typing fades sidebar, header, footer and rail together over 320ms. Returns on pointer move or Esc. Nothing is removed that you can't get back in one gesture — which is the condition for this being calming rather than alarming.
  • The chrome fades but does not collapse its width. First version slid the rail out and the prose column jumped sideways mid-sentence. Space stays reserved, only ink is removed, so the line you are writing never moves under the cursor. Worth stating explicitly because the obvious implementation gets it wrong.
  • Chapter title is edited in place in the header rather than through a modal, and no longer needs five !importants to escape the framework's input styling.

The Well

The evening sun cast long shadows across the cobblestone streets. She adjusted her coat and stepped into the alley, where the scent of old books and rain-soaked stone hung in the air. Somewhere behind her a shutter went, and then went again, and then stopped.

Nothing in the caravan moved for a long moment. Then the lead camel shifted its weight, and the whole line breathed again — a hundred animals remembering at once that they were still walking, that the walking had not in fact ended while they stood there.

He had counted the water skins twice already. Counting them a third time would not make more of them, and he knew it, and he counted them again anyway.

“How far,” Marta said. It was not a question, which was how she asked most things, and it was the reason he had hired her in the first place and the reason he now regretted it.

What changed and why
  • The 260px sidebar and 320px rail both go away. A beta reader navigates twice — once to pick a chapter, once to leave a note. Neither justifies 580px of permanent furniture around the thing they came to read.
  • Contents is a panel on demand, and it carries their own notes at the foot — which is what a reader wants to return to, and there is currently nowhere to see it.
  • Existing feedback shows as an underline in the text, not a card in a list. Click it and the note opens where the words are. The current design makes you read the quote in the rail and hunt for it in the prose.
  • The folio replaces the page bar. Chapter name, page, position in the book, set at 11px letterspaced under a 2px progress line. The current pagebar puts two chevrons and "1 / 1" in a bordered strip.
  • Page turns move to the margins. 64px hit targets down both edges at 35% opacity, full on hover — the gesture is already there as arrow keys and swipe, so the visible control can be nearly invisible.
  • 17px at 1.85 — book setting rather than UI setting, against the editor's 16/1.7. I tried justified-with-hyphenation first and reverted it: a 34em measure set in a handwriting face opens obvious rivers between words. Worth revisiting only if the prose face ever changes.
